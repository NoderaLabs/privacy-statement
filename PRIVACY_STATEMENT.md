# Skinfolio Privacy Statement

Effective date: June 16, 2026

Skinfolio is a CS2 inventory and portfolio tracking app. This privacy statement explains what information the app uses, how it is used, and how to contact us.

## What Skinfolio does

Skinfolio lets you import and track the value of a public Steam inventory, view estimated market values, monitor price changes, and use widgets for quick portfolio updates.

## Information we use

Skinfolio may use the following information to provide app functionality:

- Public Steam inventory information that you choose to import
- Steam profile identifiers or public profile URLs that you enter
- Inventory item metadata and estimated market value data
- App settings such as selected currency, widget preferences, and display options
- Portfolio data and cached snapshots stored on your device

Skinfolio is designed around public inventory tracking. A Steam login inside the app is not required.

## How information is used

We use information only to operate and improve the app, including:

- importing and displaying a public inventory
- calculating portfolio totals and price changes
- populating Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
- saving your local settings and portfolio state
- restoring your optional Supporter unlock
- diagnosing crashes or app issues when diagnostics are enabled

## Data storage

Skinfolio stores portfolio data, settings, and widget cache data on your device.

Some app data may also sync through your personal iCloud account using CloudKit, so the app can keep portfolio-related data available across your own devices. This data is stored within your Apple account environment and is not used to create a separate Skinfolio account.

## Crash and diagnostic information

Skinfolio may use Firebase Crashlytics or similar diagnostic tooling to collect crash reports and limited technical diagnostics if configured in the app build. This may include:

- app version and build number
- bundle identifier
- current screen name
- selected display currency
- portfolio scope type
- technical crash data needed to diagnose failures

Diagnostic data is used only to maintain and improve app stability.

## In-App Purchases

Skinfolio offers an optional one-time Supporter unlock through Apple In-App Purchase. Purchase processing is handled by Apple. Skinfolio does not collect or store your payment card details.

## Data sharing

Skinfolio does not sell your personal information.

Data may be processed by service providers only where needed to operate the app, such as:

- Apple services, including iCloud / CloudKit and In-App Purchase
- diagnostic providers, if enabled for crash reporting
- market data or pricing infrastructure used to estimate item values

## Children

Skinfolio is not directed to children under 13.

## Your choices

You can:

- avoid importing an inventory if you do not want to use public inventory tracking
- reset app data from within the app
- remove widgets from your device at any time
- contact us to ask privacy-related questions
